About Al Ahly SC

Al Ahly Sporting Club is one of the biggest football clubs in Africa and the Arab world. It was founded on 24 April 1907 in Cairo, Egypt.

Club History

Al Ahly was established in 1907. The club has a long history in Egyptian and African football. It has won many local and international championships.

The club is famous for its red shirt and its loyal supporters.

Our Goals

  • Winning championships.
  • Developing young football players.
  • Representing Egypt in international competitions.
  • Supporting teamwork and sportsmanship.
